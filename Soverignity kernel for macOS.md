1. PURPOSE OF THIS ADDENDUM

This Addendum establishes the MaanyaOS Project's express policy concerning interoperability with third-party operating systems, software platforms, ecosystems, application environments, hardware platforms, and proprietary technologies.

The purpose of this Addendum is to make clear that compatibility, interoperability, emulation, virtualization, translation, or integration provided by MaanyaOS is intended to facilitate lawful technical interoperability and is not intended to undermine, damage, destabilize, impersonate, unlawfully reproduce, or otherwise interfere with the sovereignty, integrity, security, ownership, or legitimate operation of another operating-system platform.

---

2. PLATFORM SOVEREIGNTY

For purposes of this License, "platform sovereignty" refers to the independent ownership, control, governance, technical architecture, security model, intellectual property, ecosystem, licensing framework, and legitimate operational authority associated with a platform and its rights holders.

MaanyaOS recognizes that third-party platforms maintain their own independent ownership and governance.

Nothing in MaanyaOS is intended to negate that independence.

---

3. macOS PLATFORM SOVEREIGNTY

MaanyaOS expressly recognizes that macOS is an independently controlled operating-system platform belonging to Apple and its applicable rights holders.

Any MaanyaOS functionality capable of interacting with, interoperating with, emulating, virtualizing, translating, or otherwise supporting software associated with macOS is intended solely as technical compatibility functionality.

Such functionality shall not be interpreted as:

- an attempt to assume ownership of macOS;
- an attempt to replace Apple's control over macOS;
- an attempt to interfere with Apple's legitimate platform governance;
- an attempt to invalidate Apple's software licensing;
- an attempt to claim Apple's proprietary technology as MaanyaOS technology;
- an attempt to represent MaanyaOS as Apple software;
- an attempt to represent MaanyaOS as an Apple operating system;
- an attempt to establish an unauthorized Apple-affiliated ecosystem; or
- an attempt to impair Apple's legitimate rights.

---

4. WINDOWS PLATFORM SOVEREIGNTY

The same principle applies to Microsoft Windows and other independently controlled platforms.

MaanyaOS compatibility functionality does not constitute an attempt to assume ownership or governance of Windows.

Microsoft retains its applicable rights in Windows and Microsoft proprietary technology.

---

5. NO HOSTILE PLATFORM INTERFERENCE

MaanyaOS compatibility technology is not intended to provide mechanisms whose purpose is to unlawfully interfere with the legitimate operation, security, availability, licensing, or governance of a third-party platform.

This includes intentional functionality designed primarily to:

- disable platform security mechanisms without authorization;
- corrupt platform installations;
- destroy platform data;
- disrupt platform services;
- damage platform infrastructure;
- interfere with legitimate licensing controls;
- impersonate platform-authorized infrastructure; or
- intentionally compromise third-party systems.

---

6. NO INTENT TO HARM

The MaanyaOS Project expressly states that its compatibility architecture is not designed or intended to harm Apple, Microsoft, Linux, GNU, Debian, Ubuntu, Arch Linux, or any other legitimate technology provider or operating-system project.

Technical interoperability should not be interpreted as hostile activity merely because two independently developed technologies interact.

---

7. NO DESTRUCTION OF THIRD-PARTY ECOSYSTEMS

MaanyaOS does not intend to destroy, disable, corrupt, or otherwise interfere with legitimate third-party software ecosystems.

Compatibility exists to enable lawful use and interoperability where technically and legally appropriate.

---

8. NO UNAUTHORIZED IMPERSONATION

MaanyaOS must not knowingly represent itself as:

- macOS;
- Windows;
- an Apple product;
- a Microsoft product;
- an Apple-authorized operating system;
- a Microsoft-authorized operating system; or
- another third-party product

unless the relevant rights holder has expressly authorized such representation.

---

9. NO FALSE ENDORSEMENT

Compatibility with a third-party platform shall not be represented as endorsement by the platform owner.

A compatibility statement describes technical functionality and does not establish a commercial relationship.

---

10. NO CLAIM OF APPLE OWNERSHIP

- macOS;
- Apple operating-system source code;
- Apple proprietary frameworks;
- Apple proprietary binaries;
- Apple firmware;
- Apple trademarks;
- Apple services; or
- other Apple intellectual property.

MaanyaOS does not claim ownership of:

12. COMPATIBILITY ≠ REPRODUCTION

The existence of compatibility functionality does not by itself establish that MaanyaOS reproduces the complete implementation of a third-party platform.

Compatibility may be achieved through independently developed mechanisms.

---

13. INDEPENDENT IMPLEMENTATION

Where MaanyaOS implements compatibility functionality independently, such implementation remains subject to applicable law and the licenses governing its individual components.

Nothing in this clause asserts that every possible compatibility implementation is legally permissible.

---

14. CLEAN INTEROPERABILITY PRINCIPLE

Where technically appropriate, MaanyaOS development may seek to maintain a separation between:

1. independently developed MaanyaOS code;
2. Open-Source components;
3. third-party proprietary software;
4. compatibility interfaces; and
5. third-party platform implementations.

This separation is intended to preserve clear intellectual-property boundaries.
